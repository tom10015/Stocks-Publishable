COMMERCIAL READINESS

Deliberately excluded

We do not ingest or republish Yahoo Finance, Stooq, Alpaca, Nasdaq calendar, FINRA short-interest, OPRA, TradingView, brokerage or other privately licensed feeds. Adding a source requires reviewing its current commercial display, derived-data and redistribution terms first.

Important licensing notes

  • The SEC says government-created site content and EDGAR public filing content are generally free to access and reuse, subject to exceptions for third-party assets. Facts and source attribution are used here; no company logos or proprietary editorial articles are included.
  • The SEC imposes fair-access rules on automated collection; the scheduled collector requires a real contact email in its request User-Agent.
  • Use of public EDGAR facts does not make a valuation or financial conclusion accurate or guaranteed. Company reporting taxonomies vary.
  • GitHub holds the code. For ads, sales, or a subscription product, deploy on a commercial-hosting service rather than treating GitHub Pages as a commercial SaaS host.
  • Company names are identifiers. No SEC endorsement or company affiliation is implied.
