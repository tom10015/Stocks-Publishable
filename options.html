MODEL NOTES

Know what this includes—and what it doesn't.

Assumes standard, non-adjusted U.S. equity options (100 shares per contract), fixed entered premiums, no early assignment, and expiration payoffs only. Does not model bid-ask spreads, commissions beyond entered fees, dividends, taxes, margin interest, early exercise, volatility changes, or option prices before expiration. Covered calls include ownership of shares and use your entered cost basis. All inputs stay in your browser. This tool is not financial advice.
